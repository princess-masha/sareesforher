1. Single-Piece Hand-Stock Policy

Every saree listed on Sarees for Her is an individual hand-stock piece. Orders are confirmed on a first-paid basis via WhatsApp. Once payment is received, the saree is marked Sold Out immediately.

Subtle irregularities in weave, thread texture, or zari work are hallmarks of authentic handloom and artisan-crafted sarees, not defects. Actual colors may vary slightly depending on screen brightness and natural lighting. You may request an unedited daylight video before ordering.

3. Shipping & Delivery

We offer free shipping across India. Confirmed orders are typically dispatched within 24–48 business hours, and tracking details are shared directly on your WhatsApp number.
